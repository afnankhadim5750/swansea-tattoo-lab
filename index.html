The studio

One client. One artist. No distractions.

Swansea Tattoo Lab is a bespoke one-on-one tattoo studio in a clinical, professional environment. Every appointment is yours alone — no rushing, no distractions, just your idea worked on properly.

Consultations cover design, placement and sizing before any ink, and aftercare instructions are provided so your tattoo heals well.
